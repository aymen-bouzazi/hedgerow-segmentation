How to use this as evidence

Check alignment, then draw visible crown parts with method colours hidden. Mark ambiguous regions separately. After drawing, compare the six frozen segmentations. Each photo uses one camera for every method.

A photograph is a partial visible reference. Points behind another crown are still projected here; there is no reliable visibility filter. Falling inside a polygon does not establish 3D tree ownership. ALS dates from February 2023; photos date from 2026. Growth, pruning, wind, foliage and sampling also matter.

Camera centres and the roughly 1.5 m camera height remain approximate. Matching errors measure relative photo agreement, not independent 3D accuracy. Draw references without method colours; earlier exposure cannot be undone, and further method exposure is recorded here. No crown accuracy or winning method is claimed.
